The one-minute brief

Customer Context Platform

Problem

Customer data exists across many systems, but product teams still reconstruct context independently. That creates duplicated logic, inconsistent personalisation and avoidable trust risk.

Vision

Make every authorised customer interaction more useful by giving products access to trusted, relevant and explainable context in real time.

Platform promise

Collect context once, govern it properly, and let every team reuse it safely.

MVP

Explicit preferences, recent intent, provenance, freshness, consent policy, customer correction and a Context API serving two activation journeys.

AI readiness

AI is used only where context quality, provenance, confidence, freshness and policy controls make activation defensible.

North Star

% of eligible customer interactions improved by trusted reusable context.
